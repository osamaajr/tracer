One list for everything you might buy.

Tracer is a free Chrome extension for the products you find while shopping but are not ready to buy. Save finds from different shops into one list instead of leaving tabs open, making another bookmark folder, or trying to remember which store had the item. When you want to look again, your saved products are together in Tracer.

To start, install Tracer in Chrome on your computer and open a product page. Open the extension from Chrome's extensions menu and choose Save to Tracer. The item appears in your saved list, with a link back to the shop. You can return to it when you are ready to decide. Tracer works with shopping pages across different stores; product details and price checks can sometimes be unavailable when a store changes its page or limits access.

Watch the prices of things you saved

Tracer can keep checking the prices of saved products and let you know when one drops. This means you can put an item aside without checking its page every day. If you buy a saved product, you can also keep watching its price after checkout. A drop alert tells you that the listed price changed so you can check the shop yourself. Tracer does not apply coupons, compare sellers, or arrange refunds.

If you are collecting options for a future purchase, start by saving one real product from a shop you use. Check that its name, image, price and link look right in your list. If a page does not capture correctly, please tell us which shop and product page caused the problem so we can improve it.
