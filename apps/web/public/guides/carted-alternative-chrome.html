Looking for a Carted alternative?

If your favourite part of Carted was keeping things you might buy from different shops in one place, you have a few options. The right one depends on whether you mostly shop on your phone, share lists, or browse in Chrome on a computer.

Start with the part you miss

Some people used Carted as a visual place for everything they were considering. Others cared most about an alert for a specific saved item when its price changed. Before moving hundreds of products into a new service, try saving a couple of real items from shops you use and check what happens when you come back to them.

Look for the exact size or colour you chose, the shop link, the price you saw, and a clear way to reopen the item. If you need to compare the same product at several retailers, save each retailer's listing separately and compare the final price, delivery and return terms at checkout.

If you use Chrome on a computer

I built Tracer for the “maybe later” products that otherwise become a pile of tabs. Open a product page, open the extension, check the detected item and price, then choose Save to Tracer. Your saved products from different shops sit in one list, and Tracer can alert you when a monitored item's price drops.

It is free and works in Chrome. You can also choose to keep watching a product after buying it. That gives you a chance to check the shop's own price-adjustment or return policy if the price falls; Tracer does not claim money back for you.

What Tracer does not replace

Tracer is a Chrome extension, not a phone app or a shared gift registry. It does not import a Carted export or sync your saved list to your phone. If mobile saving, shared lists, or automatic comparison of the same item across sellers is your main need, choose a tool built for that instead.

Price checks can be unavailable when a shop does not expose a reliable price. An alert is a cue to open the original product page, not proof of the lowest available price. Check the exact variant and full checkout cost before you buy.

A quick test: save one item from each of two shops you actually use. Reopen the list and make sure both items, links and prices are right before relying on alerts for a bigger list.

Try it with two items

If you shop in Chrome on a computer, this takes a few minutes. Install Tracer, save one product from each of two shops, and check that both landed in your list.
