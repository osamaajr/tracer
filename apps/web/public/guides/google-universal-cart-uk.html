Google Universal Cart in the UK: what can you use now?

As of 24 October 2026, Google says Universal Cart is available only in the United States. If you are in the UK, the useful question is whether you need Google’s checkout flow or simply a place to save products for later.

What Google’s cart actually does

Google lists Search, Shopping, AI Mode, and Gemini as places to add products. The cart groups saved items by merchant and can track price drops or restocks. Google launched it in the US on 15 October 2026 across Search and Gemini.

At checkout, participating merchants can accept Google Pay inside the cart. For others, Google sends you to the merchant’s site to finish. Availability of those checkout routes depends on the merchant; a saved product is not a guaranteed one-step checkout.

If you need a UK shortlist today

For a few products, make a Chrome bookmarks folder and rename each bookmark with the product and variant. For a comparison, use a note or sheet with the shop, link, price, date checked, delivery cost, and your next action. These work across retailers without waiting for a cart rollout, but you must recheck prices yourself.

Our guide to saving across shops gives the exact setup for each method.

If you want a saved list in Chrome

Open a product page, open the Tracer extension, check the detected details, and choose Save to Tracer. Your saved products appear together in the extension, and Open item takes you back to the retailer when you are ready.

Tracer can check for price drops and notify you when it verifies one, if monitoring and alerts are enabled. The list stays in your Chrome profile; it does not sync to your phone or provide checkout. Some shops do not expose a dependable price, so an alert is not guaranteed.

The key difference: Universal Cart is Google’s shopping and checkout experience. Tracer is a saved-product list in Chrome. For any change in UK availability, check Google’s availability page.

Try it with one product

Install Tracer and save one item from a UK shop you use. Check the name and price before saving, then reopen the extension to confirm it is in your list. Compare the final price, including delivery, on the retailer’s site before paying.
