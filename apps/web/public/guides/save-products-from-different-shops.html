How to save products from different shops in one place

A useful shortlist needs more than a page link. Keep the exact product variant, shop, and price you saw so you can compare the right items later. Here are three ways to do it, from a simple folder to a list with price checks.

1. Save links in a bookmarks folder

Create a folder called “Considering” in Chrome. On each product page, use the bookmark button (or ⌘D on Mac, Ctrl+D on Windows), choose that folder, and rename vague page titles to include the product and size or colour.

Choose this for a few links you simply want to reopen. A bookmark does not record the price you saw, and a shop may change or remove the page. Chrome can make bookmarks available on your other devices if you save them to your Google Account.

2. Make a comparison note

Use a note or spreadsheet when the decision depends on more than the listed price. Give each product one row with:

  • Product name, exact size or colour, shop, and link.
  • Price and date checked, plus delivery costs if known.
  • Your next action, such as “compare fit” or “wait for a sale”.

Update the price when you revisit the page. This takes manual work, but it makes it easier to compare the total cost and avoid mixing up different variants.

3. Save from the product page with Tracer

On a product page in Chrome, open Tracer from the extensions menu. Check the detected name and price before choosing Save to Tracer. Open the extension again to see your saved list; use Open item when you want to return to that shop.

Tracer keeps the list in that Chrome profile and can notify you of a verified price drop when monitoring and alerts are enabled. It does not sync the list to your phone. Some product pages do not expose a reliable price, so a check or alert may be unavailable. Always confirm the current price and delivery cost at the shop before buying.

Which should you use? Pick bookmarks for a few links, a note when you need to compare variants and total costs, or Tracer when you want a visual saved list in Chrome with possible price-drop alerts. For a shared registry or phone sync, use a tool designed for those jobs.

Try it with one item

Install Tracer and try one product page you know well. Confirm the detected details, save it, then reopen the extension to check that the item appears. You can remove it if it is not the right product.
